Portland's tree canopy is not evenly distributed, and the blocks with the least
shade are, predictably, the blocks with the most asphalt and the fewest trees.

Our team joined public canopy rasters with heat and income data to produce a map
of where a tree would do the most work. We did not win. The city's urban forestry
office asked for the export anyway, which was a better outcome than a prize.

## Reflection

I arrived to write frontend and spent most of thirty-six hours cleaning geometry.
The lesson did not take: I still volunteer for the frontend.
