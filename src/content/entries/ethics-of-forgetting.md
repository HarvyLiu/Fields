Every story in *Exhalation* is about a system that keeps running after the people
inside it have stopped thinking about why.

> The story that stayed with me is "The Truth of Fact, the Truth of Feeling,"
> which imagines a lifelog you can search — and finds that the mercy is in the
> forgetting, not the record.

I read it in the same month my family digitised twenty years of photographs, and
the coincidence did the reviewing for me. A perfect record does not produce a
perfect memory. It produces a new obligation: to curate, forever.
