Four Saturday readings, each with a bilingual reader, a table of snacks, and no
expectation that anyone sit still.

The branch had a meeting room nobody used on Saturdays and a large collection of
Spanish picture books with a sign-in rate near zero. I asked the children's
librarian why, and she said: nobody has invited them.

## The number that mattered

Average attendance went from 6 to 27 across the four sessions. The library
adopted the series into its regular calendar for the following year, which is the
version of success that outlives me.
