The judging criterion that decided it was evidence of testing with actual users.
I brought the failure log — 71% accuracy on contractions, then 94% after the
rewrite — and the judges spent most of the interview on the failure log.

## What the award did not measure

It did not measure whether the device is pleasant to hold. A student at the school
told me it was "fine," which is the most useful review the project has received.
