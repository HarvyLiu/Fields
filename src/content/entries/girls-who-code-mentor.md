Thirty Saturdays, roughly one hour each, with a rotating cohort of eight to twelve
students. My job was to be a person who did not flinch at a bug.

## What actually helped

Not the curriculum. Showing up every week was the intervention. The girls who
stayed finished their projects because they stopped being afraid of the error
message, and they stopped because someone kept reading it with them instead of
fixing it for them.

I learned to ask "what did you expect it to do?" before touching the keyboard.
I now ask that of grown-ups too.
